1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our software products, we may process the following categories of data:

Different apps may collect different data fields. The specific disclosure shown within each app shall prevail.

We only collect data that is relevant to the current app's features and operation. We do not collect information unrelated to the current app.

  • App and Device Information: device model, operating system version, language, and app version.
  • Usage Data: feature usage, session events, and interaction logs.
  • Game Data (for game apps): in-game data such as score, rank, level progress, achievements, and gameplay events.
  • Advertising Data: advertising identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from IP, ad interaction events, and related ad performance data.
  • Diagnostics and Crash Data: error logs and performance metrics to improve stability.
  • Optional User-Provided Data: information you choose to provide, such as support messages.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data unless clearly disclosed in-app and required for a feature you choose to use.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain software functionality.
  • Improve user experience and software performance.
  • Process game-related progress and leaderboard features for game apps (for example: score and rank).
  • Detect, prevent, and fix technical issues.
  • Display and measure advertisements through Google Ads and Google AdMob.
  • Respond to support requests and user feedback.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3. Legal Basis and Consent

Where required by applicable law, we process data based on your consent, contractual necessity, legitimate interests, and/or legal obligations.

4. Third-Party Services

Our software products may use third-party services (for example, analytics, crash reporting, cloud hosting, or ads). These providers may process data under their own privacy policies.

We use Google Ads and Google AdMob to serve ads, support product operations, and measure ad performance. These services may collect and process data such as advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, and interaction events according to Google's policies.

For more details, please refer to Google's privacy information: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share limited data only when necessary:

  • With service providers that support software operations, including Google Ads and Google AdMob.
  • To comply with legal requests or enforce legal rights.
  • As part of a merger, acquisition, or business transfer (with notice where required).

6. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

7. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Our software products are not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
